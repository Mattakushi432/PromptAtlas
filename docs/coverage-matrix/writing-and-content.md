- **Sub-domain**: blog/article, email, long-form/book, technical writing, UX copy, ad copy, press/PR, scriptwriting, product descriptions
- **Persona**: solo creator, marketing team, non-native-English writer, technical SME writing for laypeople
- **JTBD stage**: outline/plan → draft → edit/tighten → adapt tone → repurpose across formats → headline/hook generation
