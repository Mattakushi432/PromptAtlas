# Taxonomy

The canonical category list for PromptAtlas. Folder slugs stay in English under both `en/` and `uk/` so the two trees are mechanically comparable; only the display names are localized.

Do not fork a new subcategory until it would hold at least 3–5 real prompts. Do not add "Legal," "Financial," or "Health & Wellness" categories without a visible disclaimer at the top of every prompt in them stating the output is not professional advice.
